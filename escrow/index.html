How Mint Exchange Works

1

Create a Deal

Start by creating a deal and inviting the other party. Both parties will agree on roles: sender (buyer) or receiver (seller).

2

Fund the Escrow

The sender transfers funds to the secure escrow wallet. The funds are locked until both parties confirm completion or agree to a refund.

3

Complete the Transaction

Once the receiver delivers the product or service and the sender confirms receipt, the funds are released to the receiver.

Safety First

Automated Escrow

Mint Exchange removes the human aspect of an escrow to avoid scams. Our automated system ensures funds are securely held until all parties are satisfied.

Warning

If anyone ever asks you to deal outside of Mint Exchange, please report them immediately. We do not condone any deals outside of our platform and cannot guarantee the safety of your funds in such cases.

Zero Platform Fees

Mint Exchange charges no fees for using our service. However, network fees will apply when sending and receiving funds, which are outside of our control.
